How it works

From electricity demand to reserved clean-energy capacity.

CloudEnergy coordinates project development, generation, battery storage, energy management and contractual allocation in one operating model.

01

Demand assessment

We analyse customer load, current tariff, operating hours, location and target savings to determine an indicative capacity or fixed annual-energy requirement.

02

Project matching

Demand is matched with planned or aggregated solar, wind, hybrid and BESS assets located where generation and grid economics are strongest.

03

Long-term reservation

The customer contracts for a defined allocation and term. Commercial structures may combine an upfront reservation contribution with a lower per-unit energy charge.

04

Intelligent operation

The EMS forecasts generation and demand, coordinates battery dispatch and maintains auditable records of contracted allocations.

05

Benefits and visibility

Customers receive contracted energy benefits or eligible credits and visibility into generation, allocation, savings and environmental impact.
