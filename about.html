About CloudEnergy

Building an access layer for the next energy system.

CloudEnergy is developing a globally scalable hybrid renewable-energy, battery-storage and digital capacity-reservation platform.

The problem

Renewable access is still tied to infrastructure ownership.

Millions of consumers cannot install sufficient rooftop solar because of space, ownership, geography, financing or maintenance constraints. Large energy users face a different challenge: securing affordable clean power with dependable supply and transparent performance.

Our direction

Place infrastructure where it performs best.

Cloud Energy acquires and develops renewable and storage projects, then coordinates EPC and technology partners to deliver them and digitally maps contracted allocations to customers.
