The cost challenge

Continuous grid power is reliable—but expensive

Data centres consume electricity around the clock and generally depend on the grid for operational reliability. Their challenge is the cost of that continuous consumption and the difficulty of installing enough solar at each facility to offset it.

01

High grid expenditure

Large 24/7 compute loads create substantial monthly electricity costs and long-term tariff exposure.

02

Limited onsite space

Rooftops and nearby land are rarely sufficient to install solar capacity matching total data-centre demand.

03

Location constraints

Solar performance and available land at the facility may be weaker than at optimally located remote plants.

04

Renewable targets

Operators need measurable solar allocation and cost benefits while keeping their existing grid connection.

CloudEnergy architecture

Remote solar capacity, digitally allocated alongside grid supply.

01

Hybrid generation

Solar, wind and complementary assets can be developed or aggregated across strong-resource locations.

02

Battery storage

03

Intelligent EMS

04

Capacity reservation

Data centres digitally reserve defined solar capacity or annual generated units from remotely located plants.

Commercial direction

Reserve solar around the compute and cost roadmap.

CloudEnergy can evaluate grid consumption, annual energy requirements, current tariff and growth phases—then size a remote solar reservation designed to reduce energy expenditure while the facility remains grid connected.
